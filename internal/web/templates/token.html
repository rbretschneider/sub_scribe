How to export your cookies

  1. Install a cookies.txt browser extension (for example "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY" for Chrome or Firefox).
  2. Open youtube.com and make sure you are signed in.
  3. Click the extension and export the cookies as a cookies.txt file.
  4. Drag that file into the box below — or click to browse.

Your cookies never leave this server. They are stored with owner-only permissions and used only to talk to YouTube on your behalf.
